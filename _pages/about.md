I work at [DatologyAI](https://www.datologyai.com/) as a Member of Technical Staff, focusing on curating coding data. Previously, I was a postdoctoral fellow at [Northeastern University](https://www.northeastern.edu/), supervised by [Alina Oprea](https://www.khoury.northeastern.edu/home/alina/). I obtained my PhD from [University of Virginia](https://www.virginia.edu/), where I was extremely fortunate to be advised by Professor [David Evans](http://www.cs.virginia.edu/~evans/) <span class="funfact" tabindex="0">(fun fact)<span class="funfact-note">David initiated the concept of Systematization of Knowledge (SoK) papers — so the next time an SoK paper saves you weeks of reading, you know who to thank.</span></span>. If you're curious where I fall in the family tree, here's my [academic genealogy](https://www.cs.virginia.edu/~evans/academic-roots.html).


During my time in academia, I studied the privacy and security challenges of machine learning models, both as targets of attacks and as amplifiers of risk; exploring how models can be exploited and how they transform the security landscape of systems that use them.
